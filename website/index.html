06 / FAQTHE SHORT ANSWERS

Good to
know.

Quick answers about the collection, the desktop app, and your score.

AA?
What is Afterglow Arcade Club?+

A collection of six mini-games designed for a Windows desktop application.

What games are included?+

Perfect Circle, Krillion: The Daily Dive, Perceptle, Head Count, Draw Logos From Memory, and Contexto.

Can I play on this website?+

No. This site is informational. Gameplay belongs in the installed desktop application.

Where can I download the app?+

Download the Windows 10/11 64-bit installer from the official GitHub Release.

Is the application free?+

Pricing has not been announced.

Do I need an account?+

Create a free account to keep your player identity and sync desktop scores between installs. Guest scores stay on this device until you sign in.

How does Gamer Percentage work?+

After you have a score for all six games, the app averages your best normalized score in each. Each game counts equally.

Can I replay, and does progress save?+

Yes. The desktop app saves best scores and recent rounds locally. Sign in to sync that progress with your account across installs.

Which systems are supported?+

The current installer targets Windows 10 and 11, 64-bit. No other platform is available yet.
